01 / 05 · Your browser

Make your move.

Open the board from anywhere and choose a move. Your browser sends a small message to the website.

e2 → e4 Your opening move

02 / 05 · The secure connection

Across the internet.

Vercel checks your access, then forwards the move through the Pi’s protected Tailscale connection.

Website → Raspberry Pi

03 / 05 · Raspberry Pi

The Pi thinks ahead.

The Pi checks the rules, asks Stockfish for a reply, and saves both moves together. This is where the game lives.

Rules checked. Reply saved.

04 / 05 · The physical board

From logic to movement.

The Pi will send the move to the board’s mechanical components, which guide the real piece to its square. The position then travels back to your computer.

Pi → mechanics → piece Planned integration

05 / 05 · Back to your browser

Your next move awaits.

The confirmed position returns through the same relay. Your board updates with both moves, ready for what comes next.

e7 → e5 An example reply

One move. There and back.

The website and the Pi stay in sync over the internet. Wi-Fi, a shared laptop connection, or cellular can keep the Pi online.
