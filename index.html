How a move travels.

Every move is checked and saved on the Raspberry Pi before anything else happens.

  1. Your browser

    You make a move from anywhere, on any device.

  2. Secure relay

    A signed session and a private tunnel carry it to the board.

  3. Raspberry Pi

    The Pi validates the move and saves the game.

  4. Stockfish

    The engine replies at the level you picked.

  5. The board moves

    The Pi plans every piece’s path. Connecting the motors is the next stage of the build.
